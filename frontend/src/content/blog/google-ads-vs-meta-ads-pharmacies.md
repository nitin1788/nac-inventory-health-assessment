Once [local SEO and Google Business Profile](/blog/local-seo-for-pharmacies-medical-stores-guide) are in place, the next question most pharmacy owners run into is whether to put money into Google Ads or Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) — and most advice just says "do both," without explaining why, or what to do first if budget doesn't stretch to both at once.

## 1. They capture two different moments

**Google Ads** shows up when someone is already actively searching — "pharmacy near me," "where to get a flu shot," "24-hour medical store." That's high-intent traffic: the person has already decided they want something, and you're competing to be the answer.

**Meta Ads** works earlier than that — reaching people based on where they live and what they're interested in, before they've necessarily started searching for anything. It's better suited to building awareness of a service, a promotion, or your store's existence in the area, not capturing someone mid-search.

## 2. Which to start with depends on what's already in place

If your Google Business Profile and local SEO are genuinely solid, you're likely already capturing a good share of the "already searching" traffic organically — in that case, Meta Ads can make more sense as the next step, building broader awareness rather than competing with your own organic visibility. If your local presence isn't there yet, or you're in a market where competitors are already bidding heavily on the searches that matter, Google Ads can be the more urgent gap to close, because every search you don't show up for is a customer going to a competitor who does.

One general industry figure worth knowing: Meta's Lead Ads have been reported to run at roughly a third of the cost-per-lead of Google Ads for service businesses generally — not pharmacy-specific, and not something to treat as a guaranteed outcome, but a reasonable data point if budget is the deciding factor.

## 3. Eventually, the goal is both, coordinated

Running Google Ads and Meta Ads as two separate, disconnected campaigns leaves money on the table — the two work better managed together, with one budget logic and one set of reporting, which is the whole premise behind [PPC & Performance Marketing](/services/digital-marketing/ppc-performance-marketing). Starting with one doesn't mean staying there; it means being deliberate about where the first rupee of ad spend does the most good.

## 4. Whichever you start with, make sure it actually gets measured

A campaign on either platform is only as useful as your ability to see what it's actually producing — including [WhatsApp enquiries](/blog/whatsapp-conversion-tracking-pharmacy), which neither platform tracks correctly without extra setup. Get [Analytics & Conversion Tracking](/services/digital-marketing/analytics-conversion-tracking) right before scaling spend on either channel, not after.
